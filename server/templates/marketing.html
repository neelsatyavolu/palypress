Your Home feed

One feed, or one masthead.

Home can show every publication together or a single newsroom. Filter by section, follow the ones you care about, and get a Monday digest by email.

  • All PALY Publications — every newsroom in one Home feed, or switch to a single masthead.
  • Notifications by publication — turn alerts on or off per masthead, including category-level options.
  • Weekly digest email — top stories of the week, every Monday.
  • Category chips — filter News, Spotlight, Sports, Features, and more.
Local tab

City hall, campus, community.

Local News gathers government, elections, and community coverage from across Paly’s newsrooms so district and city stories are easy to find.

  • Cross-publication — Campanile, Verde, Voice, and more in one local stream.
  • Clear badges — each card shows which newsroom published it.
  • Save & share — bookmark or share from the card, same as Home.
